# Japan Rule — Kyoto lodging consultation preparation pack

Prototype v0.1 · Checked: 2026-09-07 · English sample

## Example project

Fictional scenario: an overseas operator is considering converting an existing building in Kyoto City into a small lodging facility under the Hotel Business Act. Address, plans, capacity and staffing are not supplied. This pack prepares first consultations; it does not determine a permit category, property eligibility or permission to open.

## Preparation and questions

Collect the address, current building use, floor area by storey, available building records, planned alterations, guest capacity and staffing plan. This is our proposed consultation input list, not a statutory application checklist.

| ID | Public-source finding | Question to resolve / next action |
|---|---|---|
| K01 | Kyoto directs applicants to check zoning and possible district-plan/building-agreement restrictions. [S1 §1(3)] | Give the city building/planning contact the address and ask which location restrictions affect the plan. |
| K02 | Existing-building conversions must comply with relevant building standards even when a confirmation procedure is unnecessary. [S2 existing-building section] | Ask an architect and city officials which procedures and alterations the specific plans require under current rules. |
| K03 | The community-harmony procedure covers specified construction and changes of use, with exclusions and area-dependent procedures. [S3 §1] | Ask the Building Guidance Division whether this project falls within scope and which area procedure applies. |
| K04 | For in-scope projects, signs are placed before the 90-day point preceding building confirmation application, or the 50-day point preceding lodging-permit application if confirmation is unnecessary. [S3 §2(2)] | Confirm scope and the applicable dates before setting a schedule. These periods are not total permit-processing times. |
| K05 | Kyoto directs operators to consult related building, fire and other authorities. [S1 §1(2)] | Take plans and capacity to the relevant fire authority to establish checks and documents. This sample does not assess equipment requirements. |
| K06 | City guidance addresses identity checks and emergency-response arrangements. [S1 operations sections] | Ask lodging reviewers to assess the proposed staffing/check-in arrangement. No remote-operation approval is implied. |

## Suggested consultation order

Editorial preparation order, not a statutory sequence: collect property facts → discuss location/building feasibility and lodging category → clarify community procedures and scheduling → develop fire-safety and operating arrangements → confirm application documents. Some consultations may run in parallel.

Initial lodging contact: Kyoto City Medical and Sanitation Center, lodging review, 075-746-7209. Appointments are requested before visits. [S1 §1(1)] English-language assistance has not been verified.

## Consultation brief

“We are assessing [address] for lodging use. Current use: [ ]; area/storeys: [ ]; proposed works: [ ]; guests: [ ]; staffing and check-in: [ ]; target date: [ ]. Please identify the consultation route and documents needed to assess the plan. Zoning, building procedures, community-procedure scope and permit category remain unconfirmed.”

## Unresolved matters

K01–K06 all remain open. This is not a complete opening checklist. Property rights, incorporation/immigration, tax, food service, employment, insurance, contracts, accessibility details and detailed building/fire design are not assessed. Changes to location, works, capacity or operation require renewed review. No project cost or opening date is estimated.
