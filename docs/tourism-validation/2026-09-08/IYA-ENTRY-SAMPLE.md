# Iya Small Lodging — Entry Preparation Pack

Prototype 0.2 · 2026-09-08 · English customer sample (contact clarification after v0.1 comparison)

## Free preview: explore the opportunity

Iya, in Miyoshi City, is a candidate for a small lodging concept centred on traditional buildings and local experiences. The city's tourism site lists converted traditional-house accommodation. This demonstrates an existing business format, not unmet demand or a guarantee of profitability. [S1]

Miyoshi's vacant-house programme is a starting point for property enquiries. A listing does not establish commercial lodging eligibility or permission to alter a building. [S2]

For this prototype, the business concept is a small whole-house stay. Before choosing a property, investigate guest access, seasonal operations, local staffing and maintenance. These are our business-planning questions; this pack does not supply verified transport schedules, occupancy, property prices or renovation estimates.

## Paid-content sample: prepare the first consultations

### Fictional input

- Area: Iya, Miyoshi City; exact address not supplied.
- Building: existing detached dwelling; area, storeys and records unknown.
- Concept: one guest group, up to six guests, accommodation only, no meals or tours.
- Intended route for discussion: Hotel Business Act lodging; category not determined.
- Renovation, operator presence and target opening date: undecided.

### Result

**Status: consultation preparation only.** Location and building data are insufficient for a property-specific eligibility decision. Gather an address, plans, current-use records, proposed works and an operating outline before consultations. The six-guest assumption does not establish a permit category.

### Contact routes

- Lodging: Miyoshi Public Health Center, environmental hygiene. The [prefectural FAQ](https://www.pref.tokushima.lg.jp/FAQ/docs/00010047/) lists **0883-72-1122**. Earlier national-directory search results gave 0883-72-1121; this discrepancy is unresolved. Consult the prefectural page for routing; neither number has been telephone-tested.
- Building: Miyoshi-area building consultation, **0883-76-0609**, listed in prefectural guidance. Older documents use the former regional-bureau name; confirm the current department when contacting. [S4]
- Fire: the [regional fire organisation directory](https://www.miyoshikouiki.jp/soshiki/) lists the Prevention Division at **0883-76-5119**. Ask it to route the exact address; station-specific jurisdiction is not established here. [S5]
- Heritage/property: begin with Miyoshi City through its official site and vacant-house contact route. [S2, S6]

Web-listed contacts only; telephone validity, response times and English-language support have not been tested. No office has reviewed this fictional project.

Please advise which consultation route and documents are needed to assess the plan. The permit category, building/fire procedures and preservation-district status are unconfirmed.”

### Suggested next actions

1. Obtain property facts and owner consent information.
2. Discuss lodging, building and fire questions; these consultations may overlap.
3. Establish any heritage procedure before committing to alterations.
4. Obtain work and operating estimates, then reconsider the business plan.

This is an editorial preparation sequence, not a statutory approval sequence. Revisit the pack if address, guest capacity, works, meals or operation change. Company formation, immigration, tax, contracts and labour arrangements are outside this prototype. No complete opening checklist, investment return or opening approval is supplied.
